If you mean **“LLM hacks”** as practical techniques for getting better results from large language models, here are some high-value ones:

 - **Give the model a role + goal:** “Act as a senior product manager. Help me…”
- **Provide context before the task:** Include audience, constraints, examples, and what “good” looks like.
- **Ask for structured output:** “Return a table with columns X, Y, Z.”
- **Use examples (few-shot prompting):** Show 1–3 examples of the desired input → output pattern.
- **Ask for alternatives:** “Give me 3 approaches and explain the trade-offs.”
- **Make it critique itself:** “First draft the answer, then identify weaknesses and improve it.”
- **Separate reasoning from the deliverable:** Ask it to analyze the problem and then produce a concise final answer.
- **Use iterative prompting:** Draft → critique → revise is often better than one giant prompt.
- **Give explicit constraints:** “Under 150 words, no jargon, aimed at a beginner.”
- **Ask it to identify uncertainty:** “Distinguish facts, assumptions, and things you’re unsure about.”
- **For coding, provide tests first:** Ask for expected behavior/test cases before implementation.
- **For research, demand sources:** Ask it to distinguish sourced facts from inference and verify current information when necessary.
